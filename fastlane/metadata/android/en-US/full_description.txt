QRieux is a simple, fast QR code and barcode scanner designed for everyone.

Features:
• Instant QR code and barcode scanning
• Create QR codes — generate QR codes for websites, emails, phone numbers, Wi-Fi, and text
• Scan from gallery — pick any image with a QR code
• Share text, URLs, contacts, or images to the app — create or scan QR codes instantly
• One-tap actions: open URLs, call phone numbers, send emails, add contacts, connect to WiFi
• Copy and share scanned content easily
• Flash/torch support for low-light scanning
• Clean, easy-to-use interface with large buttons

No ads. No tracking. Works fully offline. Just scan and create.

QRieux is open source: https://github.com/abumalick/QRieux
