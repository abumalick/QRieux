QRieux is a simple, fast QR code and barcode scanner designed for everyone.

Features:
• Instant QR code and barcode scanning
• Scan from gallery — pick any image with a QR code
• Share images to the app — scan QR codes shared from other apps
• One-tap actions: open URLs, call phone numbers, send emails, connect to WiFi
• Copy and share scanned content easily
• Flash/torch support for low-light scanning
• Clean, easy-to-use interface with large buttons

No ads. No tracking. Works fully offline. Just scan.
